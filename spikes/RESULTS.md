- **Shape:** the full gated loop of spec §6.1 passed as designed. No step of the decision rule was needed; `update_plan` and `set_hypotheses` stay.
- **Model:** `muse-spark-1.3`, reasoning effort `medium`.

- **Finding 1 (fixed here, carry to D5):** the model cannot read `call_id` back from replayed history. Tool outputs must show it: `{"callId", "result"}`. Before the fix, 12 of 12 refusals were callId guesses and no run could cite tool evidence; after it, every run cited the `executeTest` result.
- **Finding 2 (D5):** models first write field paths as `result.success`. One refusal per run, self-corrected next turn. D5: say paths are relative to `result` in the prompt, or accept a leading `result.`.
- **Finding 3 (D5):** the `connector-errors` description in the contract names the failure ("Green Liar"), which hints at the answer. Consider neutral tripwire descriptions.
- **Cost:** about $0.08 per run at ~120k input tokens (~70% cached). The preview is billed on this account.
